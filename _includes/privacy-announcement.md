<h2>Meta<sup>&reg;</sup> brand and all other Smart Glasses are PROHIBITED</h2>
<br>
<h3>If you have any such device in your possession, please do yourself a favor: return to your car or hotel room, and leave those devices there.  Anyone observed wearing or operating such devices on the floor of the conference will be asked to deactivate them and remove them from the conference immediately.  Failure to comply may result in revocation of conference credential (badge) and ejection from the conference.</h3>
<br>
<h4>As announced on social media earlier this year:</h4>
<p>
The Rochester Security Summit values the safety and privacy of its attendees, staff, presenters, sponsors, and volunteers.
Therefore, we recently updated our rules and our Code of Conduct, which you can see on our website.
	
We feel it's important to emphasize one element of these rules for all before anyone arrives at the conference with incorrect expectations and we have a difficult situation:
Glasses such as the Meta Ray-Bans and all similar items that have an operating mode of recording audio and/or video as the wearer goes about, are not allowed to be operated within the Security Summit.
	
Anyone observed operating one of these devices at the Summit will be asked to deactivate it and put it in their car or hotel room. Thank you for your understanding. 
Ensuring the safety and privacy of everyone at the Summit is our priority.
</p>
